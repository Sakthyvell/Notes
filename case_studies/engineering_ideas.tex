\subsection{Refactoring a Django Application}
Refactoring a large,legacy Django application was my task in one of the projects. The application had grown over time and had become difficult to maintain. I do not think of this as a bad codebase since this is normal for many mature projects. Small projects starts with focusing on features and speed of delivery. Over time, as the codebase grows, it becomes harder to maintain without proper structure and design patterns.
